Calculation source

On open, evaluate po_rows[] → segs[]; do not use truncated alerts[].

Date rule

Actual present: actual − plan. Otherwise: report − plan. No plan: do not evaluate.

Event identity

Deduplicate by PO + style + stage + plan + actual before display and statistics.

Historical ownership

A completed late stage belongs only to its historical stage pop-up, not the later current stage.

Boundaries

CRD-only lateness, missing-plan rows, and empty results do not create an overdue area. Delay reasons are excluded.
